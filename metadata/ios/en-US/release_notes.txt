Look after your Mac from your phone:
• Storage: see free space and clean up caches, logs, Xcode data and the Trash
• Memory and CPU: see what slows the Mac down and quit an app
• Power: keep the display on, switch lid-closed mode
• Notifications when disk space or memory runs low
• Promo Studio: free-form videos, exactly as you describe them, choosing the cover frame and Promo Templates video templates
The Mac features need Remote Code 1.5 on the Mac.

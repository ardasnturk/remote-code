Extensions, with Remote Code 1.8 on your Mac:
• Settings → Extensions lists your tools: install them on your Mac, update them, or switch them off
• MRRadar tab: subscription analytics, MRR forecasts and alerts for your RevenueCat apps
• FunnelRadar tab: funnel leaks, retention and active users for your Mixpanel projects
• Their alerts reach your phone, and tapping one opens that project

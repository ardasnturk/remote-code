## Remote Code 1.7.0

**App Review notifications.** With Vitrin on your Mac, Remote Code tells you when one of your apps is waiting for review, in review, approved, on the App Store or rejected. You get it on the Mac and, through ntfy, on your phone. Tap it on the phone to open that app in the Vitrin tab (iPhone and iPad, build 26).

Remote Code looks at your apps in App Store Connect every five minutes while the Mac is awake, so a change can take up to five minutes to reach you, and a Mac that was asleep tells you when it wakes. Turn notifications on in Settings → Notifications; **App Review status** in the same place turns this one off. Needs Vitrin 2.1.0 or later on the Mac.

**The Vitrin tab stops waiting** (iPhone and iPad, from 1.6.2). Pages open from a cache on your phone, each says how old it is, and you can search apps, projects and long lists.

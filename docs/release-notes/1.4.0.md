## Remote Code 1.4.0

### Promo Studio tells you when it's done

* **Notifications** — when [Promo Studio](https://github.com/ardasnturk/promo-studio)
  1.1 runs on your Mac, Remote Code tells you when a video is ready, a
  storyboard waits for your approval, a post went out on Instagram or a job
  failed: on the Mac and, through ntfy, on your phone — also while the phone
  app is closed. Tapping the notification opens the video in the Promo Studio
  tab. Choose which ones you get in Settings → Notifications → Promo Studio.
* **Remaining time** — videos in production show how long they still need in
  the Promo Studio tab ("About 3 min left").

The notifications need the new Mac app; the remaining time and opening a video
from a notification need the new iPhone or iPad app.

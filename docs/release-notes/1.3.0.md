## Remote Code 1.3.0

### Promo Studio on your iPhone

If [Promo Studio](https://github.com/ardasnturk/promo-studio) runs on the Mac,
the iPhone and iPad app shows a **Promo Studio** tab: your projects, videos
from your own prompt, storyboards to approve or revise, video playback,
captions, posting and scheduling to Instagram, and Instagram sign-in. It talks
to Promo Studio through the Mac app over the same end-to-end encrypted
connection; nothing goes through a server. Without Promo Studio the tab stays
hidden and nothing changes.

### Fixed

* **Settings opens in front.** Settings… in the menu bar now brings the
  Settings window to the front instead of opening it behind the app you were
  using.

The Promo Studio tab needs the new Mac app and the new iPhone or iPad app.

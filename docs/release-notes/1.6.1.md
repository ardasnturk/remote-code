## Remote Code 1.6.1

**Lid-closed mode on battery.** When the mode is paused because your Mac is on battery, the Mac menu now shows **Use on battery too** right under the notice. One click turns the pause off and keeps your Mac awake with the lid closed.

A closed MacBook that stays awake can get warm and drain its battery, so keep it ventilated. You can turn the pause back on in Settings → Lid-closed mode.

This update is for the Mac app. The iPhone and iPad app doesn't need to change.

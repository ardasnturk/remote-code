## Remote Code 1.6.2

iPhone and iPad only (build 25). The Mac app doesn't change, and Vitrin 2.1.0 or later on the Mac is enough.

**The Vitrin tab stops waiting.** What it reads from App Store Connect is now kept on your phone, per account, so pages open at once and most need no request. Older results show right away and update behind the page. Each page says how old it is; pull down or tap the refresh button to read it again.

**Search in the Vitrin tab.** Find apps, projects and team pages from the top of the tab, and filter long lists such as builds, reviews, testers and users in place.

The cached App Store Connect data stays on your phone. Clear Cached Transcripts in Settings and unpairing a Mac delete it.

## Remote Code 1.5.0

### Look after your Mac from your phone

When the Mac is far away, a full disk or a memory hog can stall every agent.
1.5.0 lets you see and fix that from your iPhone or iPad.

* **Storage** (Settings → Storage) — free space on the Mac and a cleanup of
  files that are safe to delete: app caches, logs, Xcode build data, package
  caches (npm, pnpm, Gradle, Cargo, Homebrew, …), downloaded installers and the
  Trash. Pick categories or single items; they go to the Trash unless you
  choose **Delete Permanently**. The phone never sends a file path — it can
  only pick from the Mac's own list.
* **Memory and CPU** (Settings → Memory and CPU) — memory pressure, processor
  load and the apps using the most, with helper processes counted in their
  app. Swipe to **Quit** or **Force Quit** an app.
* **Power** (Settings → Power) — keep the Mac's display on for a while, and
  switch lid-closed mode and "pause while on battery" from the phone.
* **Dark screen behind a closed lid** — in lid-closed mode the built-in
  display is now darkened while the lid is closed, so it no longer stays lit
  (and warm) in your bag. On by default.
* **New notifications** — "Low disk space" below 10 GB and "Memory almost
  full", naming the app that uses the most. Tapping one opens the matching
  screen. Both are on by default (Settings → Notifications on the Mac).

### Promo Studio: free-form videos

* A new video type next to the promo film and the quick template: **Free-form
  video**. Claude makes exactly the video you describe, without advertising
  the app; the app appears only where your prompt asks for it. Needs
  [Promo Studio](https://github.com/ardasnturk/promo-studio) 1.2.0 on the Mac
  (older versions make a promo film instead).
* **Choose the cover** in a post's Share tab: scrub through the video and pick
  the frame. It becomes the cover in Promo Studio and on Instagram. Needs
  Promo Studio 1.2.2.
* **Templates** — the Mac's template libraries, such as Promo Templates, now
  appear under Templates. Run a video template with your own request, have
  Claude research trending ideas for them, and see the videos made from the
  library. Needs Promo Studio 1.2.4.
* **Videos without a project** — the **+** button makes a video from a
  prompt (Claude directs it, about no app) or from a template. Picking a
  project and an Instagram account is optional, and any account can go with
  any project. Each post can also choose its own account.
* **Save to Photos** and **Share** for every rendered video, so you can post
  it yourself.
* **New Template…** — describe a video template and Claude Code adds it to
  the library on the Mac. Needs Promo Studio 1.3.0 (as do the two above).

Disk cleanup, the activity list and the dark-screen mode are adapted from
[Perch](https://github.com/mfrkankaya/perch) by Furkan Kaya (MIT).

The Mac features need both the new Mac app and the new iPhone or iPad app;
free-form videos and the cover picker need only the new iPhone or iPad app.

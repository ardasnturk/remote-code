## Remote Code 1.8.0

**Extensions.** Settings on your Mac and iPhone now list Promo Studio, Vitrin, MRRadar and FunnelRadar. See what's installed and which version you have, install one or update it, and switch off its tab and alerts. Remote Code installs a download only after its checksum, developer signature and Gatekeeper check pass.

**Two new extensions.**

* [MRRadar](https://mrradar.ardasnturk.com) puts your RevenueCat apps on one radar: MRR forecasts, month-end pace, quick ratio and alerts.
* [FunnelRadar](https://funnelradar.ardasnturk.com) does the same for Mixpanel: funnel leaks, retention, stickiness and alerts.

Each gets a tab on your iPhone and iPad, and its alerts reach your phone through ntfy (build 27). Turn them on in Settings → Notifications with **Extension alerts**.

With every extension on, iOS puts the last tab under More.

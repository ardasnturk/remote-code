## Remote Code 1.5.2 (iOS)

Promo Studio from the phone, with Promo Studio 1.5.0 on the Mac:

* **Share as** Reel, Post, Carousel or Story when posting or scheduling. A
  9:16 image can only be a story.
* **Carousels**: swipe through the slides in the preview; Save to Photos and
  Share take all of them.
* **Slides** for a new image post: a single image or a carousel of 2–10.
* With an older Promo Studio, picture posts stay save-and-post-yourself, and
  the app says to update the Mac.

The Mac app is unchanged; it stays at 1.5.1.

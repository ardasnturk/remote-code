## Remote Code 1.5.1

### Promo Studio from the phone

Needs Promo Studio 1.4.0 on the Mac.

* **Errors you can act on.** A failed step shows what happened in plain words
  and the fix as a button: **Try Again** (runs only the failed step), **Let
  Claude Fix It** (when the render stops on an error in the video's code),
  **Produce Without Voiceover** or **Reconnect Account**. The raw output is
  under **Details**. The alerts from the Mac carry the same buttons.
* **Mac Status**: Promo Studio's setup checklist on the phone, including disk
  space and Instagram sessions about to expire. The Promo Studio tab tells you
  when the Mac needs attention, and says clearly when Promo Studio isn't open
  on the Mac or is too old for a feature.
* **Your photos**: add photos from the photo library to a new video; Claude
  looks at them and builds the video around them.
* **Image posts**: New Video can make a single picture (4:5, 1:1 or 9:16).
  Approve the design, then save it to Photos and post it from Instagram.
* **People's photos**: videos show real photos of the people in them. Choose
  between the downloaded photo and Codex's edit, or have Codex edit it again
  with your own words.

### Mac

* An **Image ready** notification for Promo Studio's image posts. It follows
  the "Video ready" setting.
